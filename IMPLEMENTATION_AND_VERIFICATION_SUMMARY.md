## Changes made in response to the reviewers

1. The manuscript distinguishes the unconditional waiting-time law, including the day-zero flare mass, from the law conditional on a transient current state, and Figure 2 plots both.
2. The simulator, equations, and HMM likelihood use the same distributions: fresh patient-day wearable noise, jointly observed log-normal laboratory panels, and no laboratory-density term when no panel is drawn.
3. HMM fitting uses latent-state Baum-Welch expectation maximization rather than simulator labels. Three K-means initializations with different random states are run and every likelihood trace is archived; in this well-separated regime all three reach the same solution.
4. Parameter variation is propagated through 16 responsibility-conditioned draws plus the EM estimate for each HMM; the event-history model uses 16 patient-bootstrap refits plus its base fit.
5. No-flare patients remain in the censored evaluation, finite PMFs retain the `>180` tail, and uncertainty resamples simulation seeds and then patients.
6. The draw, no-draw, draw-stratified-emission, and event-history models all produce the same time-to-flare PMF schema and are compared with proper time-to-event scores.
7. The event-history comparator initializes pre-panel laboratory history from the training cohort, not from a generating parameter, and includes an explicit prior-panel indicator.
8. Complete PMFs, fitted HMM ensembles, event-history coefficients, scalers, and bootstrap members, landmark-level predictions, and patient-level metrics are archived.
9. A non-current-flare sensitivity analysis is reported separately from the all-landmark primary analysis.
10. The latent-Markov, informative-dropout, and shared-parameter literature requested by Reviewer 2 is discussed in Related Work.

## How the release is verified

`make reproduce` runs, in order: the unit tests, the full simulation, the manuscript build, release metadata, the checksum manifest, and `verify_release.py`. The verifier:

- checks the run configuration against the values stated in Appendix B;
- checks that every archived PMF sums to one including its tail and that its day-zero mass equals the filtered flare probability;
- checks the counts and contents of every archived HMM ensemble and event-history bootstrap ensemble;
- recomputes the event-history pre-panel reference from the regenerated training cohort;
- regenerates every inferential table from the archived landmark and patient files and compares them numerically;
- regenerates the manuscript TeX from the archived outputs and compares it byte for byte with the TeX used for the PDF;
- checks that the compiled PDF is a PDF and reports the regenerated primary log score;
- cross-checks `ARTIFACTS.csv`, the release tag, the DOI configuration, and every checksum in `MANIFEST.sha256`.

It prints `Release verification passed (submission mode).` on success.
